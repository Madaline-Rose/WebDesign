Myths vs Reality

Myth

Black cats bring bad luck.

Reality

In many cultures, black cats are symbols of luck, protection, and prosperity.

Myth

Black cats are connected to witchcraft.

Reality

These beliefs originated from fear and superstition during medieval times.

Support Black Cat Adoption

Black cats are often overlooked in shelters because of long-standing superstitions.

In reality, they are just as affectionate, playful, and unique as any other cat.

Choosing to adopt a black cat helps challenge outdated myths while giving an animal a loving home.
